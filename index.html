Six friends, one ritual.

What started as a simple idea between six friends soon became a shared vision — to create glass tumblers that blend style, quality, and everyday functionality. Inspired by a love for aesthetic living and thoughtful design, we set out to craft products that make every sip feel a little more special.

Founded by Naissha Porwal, Khusshi Parekh, Nami Mehta, Dev Popat, Divyanshu Jain, and Bhumit Hoondlani, our brand is built on creativity, collaboration, and the belief that everyday essentials should be both beautiful and practical.
